    About Taqadum

    Taqadum Int is a strategic consulting corporation with the main office based in Tripoli-Libya and branches overseas. The focus of Taqadum Int is on serving corporate, government, and social institutes with the highest level of quality and professionalism. Our strength comes from an experienced team of national and international experts working with inclusive collaborative spirits. Our consultancy aims to help the most ambitious change-makers define the future. We work alongside our clients as one team with a shared ambition to achieve extraordinary results, outperform the competition, and redefine industries.

    Our strength comes from an experienced team of national and international experts working with inclusive collaborative spirits. We are committed to achieving extraordinary results, outperforming the competition, and redefining industries.

    Our Vision

    To be recognized as the leading provider of strategic consulting for the development and growth of both corporate and social institutes across Libya.

    Our Mission

    To provide all the necessary resources and support tools to help our clients achieve their goals and objectives. We are recognized for providing innovative, best-in-class consulting solutions and services.

    Our Values

    At Taqadum Int, we are committed to a set of core values that define who we are and drive our operations.

    Social Responsibility

    Taqadum Int invests heavily in the wellbeing, safety, and sustainability of all interacting internally or externally with our operations to ensure a healthy environment.

    Integrity

    The reputation of Taqadum Int depends and thrives on trust, integrity, openness, and fairness at all levels of the organization and with clients.

    Mutual Benefit

    Taqadum Int’s success comes second because it relies on the Client’s interests and needs paramount, focusing on a relationship that brings value to all parties involved.

    Commitment & Accountability

    Taqadum Int believes in owning full responsibility to achieve success, with full authority and accountability for doing the right thing in the right way regardless of the cost.


    Our Team

    They say it, but Taqadum Int means it: our team is the secret to our success. Taqadum Int is fortunate to accommodate a team of experts with the highest quality of professionalism in their various fields of work both domestically and internationally.

    The team’s success isn’t solely originated by the knowledge they all hold but more importantly the hands-on experience through many successful projects they have accomplished. Our team is a talented tight-knit group with a common vision of delivering excellent results for our clients consistently and ensuring a fun, inclusive, challenging workplace with a rewarding career.

    We are proud to present a diverse team of national and international experts to build the bridge of success through the in-depth understanding of the Libyan social, political, and economical context together with the global perspectives.

    Our Services

    Corporate Consultancy Services

    The Libyan authorities encourage the setting-up of foreign operations that offer back-office administration and support services to other foreign enterprises. Libya is in a good position to encourage operations of this nature because of the availability of a well-educated workforce. Added important advantages are the very moderate and comparatively low office and employment costs. The availability of qualified and competent staff is vital for a back-office operation to flourish.

    TVET Consultancy Services

    Providing innovative solutions to government departments and educational institutions in Libya.

    Taqadum INT provides consultancy services for government departments, educational institutions, and associations in the field of Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) in Libya. This valuable service helps transform the lives of individuals and communities through the combination of academic theory and practical, hands-on vocational training.

    Design and Implementation of Training Programs
    Development or Revision of Available Curricula
    Review of available training material and development of new training material.
    Support in the planning and procurement of training hardware, including industrial components, and the upskilling of trainers
    Planning and setup of training centers – from train ing hardware to the definition of new qualification programs
